# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free collaboration space for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or viewpoint.

## Expected behaviour

- Be constructive, professional and evidence-oriented.
- Respect differing methodological opinions and discuss their assumptions openly.
- Give credit for ideas, code, data and research contributions.
- Protect confidential information, credentials and data subject to licence restrictions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, disclosure of private information, or knowingly submitting unlicensed or confidential material are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may remove content, moderate discussion or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and the project.
