# Cover letter draft

Dear Editors,

We submit the manuscript **“Random Matrix Filtering and Planar Financial Networks: A Retrospective Assessment for Volatility Forecasting in Complex Financial Markets”** for consideration as a regular research article in *Chaos, Solitons & Fractals*.

The manuscript studies Brazilian equities traded on B3 as a complex financial system. We connect four established perspectives in one reproducible design: empirical dependence diagnostics, Random Matrix Theory spectral separation, matched MST/PMFG network representations, and a chronological volatility-model assessment. The central methodological contribution is a controlled comparison of the original market structure with the topology that remains after the broad market mode is removed. This design makes changes in collective synchronization, localized clustering, hub organization and subsector connectivity directly comparable.

The paper is deliberately cautious about prediction. Structural descriptors are estimated from the complete synchronized sample, so the model experiment is reported as a retrospective structural association rather than a real-time forecasting gain. The manuscript therefore distinguishes established algorithms from the new empirical integration and provides a reproducible basis for a future point-in-time experiment.

The work fits the journal’s interests in complex networks, fluctuations and random processes, artificial intelligence and machine learning, social-science applications, and econophysics. The analysis software and configuration files are publicly available in the accompanying repository:

https://github.com/dreyvinixz/RMT_Planar_Networks_Volatility_Forecasting_Brazil

The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have reviewed and approved the submitted version and agree with its submission to the journal.

Thank you for your consideration.
